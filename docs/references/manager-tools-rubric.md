## Purpose

- The resume exists to get an interview. It says what you did and how well, as efficiently as possible: the headlines, not the story. [MT: Stinks, RU21, Managers]
- Test every proposed change against that purpose. Most outside resume advice fails the test. [MT: RU21, RU22]
- **Gatekeeper exception:** when a hiring manager, a recruiter who will submit you, or the target organization asks for a different format, length, or section, and the request is ethical, comply for that application. [MT: RU20, RU22]

## Structure

- **One page.** With 10+ years of experience, expect to cut accomplishments you're proud of. If the page isn't full, add. [MT: RU20, Mistakes]
- **Reverse chronological.** No functional or combined format. [MT: Stinks, RU22]
- **Omit:** an objective, summary, headline, or branding statement; a skills list; a certifications section; a keyword paragraph; hobbies; a references line. [MT: RU22, RU20, Stinks, Mistakes, Sample]
- **Header:** name, then one line of contact details: city and state, email, website. No phone number. [Kyle]
- **Education after experience:** degree, school, dates. [MT: Sample]
- **Continuing education:** list an item only if it's directly relevant to the target role, still relevant if older than about two years, and widely recognized in the industry. Three items at most. A course shows learning, not ability. [MT: RU20, Stinks]
- **Certifications:** No certifications on any resume, tailored variants included. A certification the posting requires goes in the cover letter. [MT: Stinks]
- **Tailored variants** follow every rule here. They vary only which accomplishments appear. [MT: Accomplishments, Myths]

## Job Entry Format

```
[Mon YYYY] – [Mon YYYY]: [Title], [Company]
[Responsibilities paragraph: short prose on scope]

- [Accomplishment bullet]
- [Accomplishment bullet]
```

- **Admin line on every role:** start and end month and year, title, company. Years alone read as concealment. Replace "present" when you leave. Condensed earlier roles follow the same rule. [MT: Stinks, RU20, Experience, Mistakes]
- **Set off dates, titles, and company names** in bold; readers scan those first. [MT: Myths, Mistakes] No underline, though MT adds one. [Kyle]
- **Several roles at one employer:** give each role its own entry and repeat the company name. Split only when the title or duties changed. After a rename or acquisition, write "New Name (formerly Old Name)" on the first role under the new name. Show growth within the company, including work beyond the job description before a promotion. [MT: Multiple Jobs, Stinks]
- **Use the common title** for the work, so searches and readers recognize it. [MT: Myths]
- **Responsibilities paragraph:** the job description anyone in the role would share, written tight with no padding. Fill it with what the hiring manager needs: customers, team and size, budget, project size, scope. Numbers that describe scope belong here; results don't. Work unique to you goes in a bullet with a measure, not here. Describe the highest-level work you did by the end of the role. [MT: Experience, Stinks, Multiple Jobs]
- **Bullets hold accomplishments only,** never responsibilities. [MT: Experience, Stinks]
- **No fixed bullet count,** per entry or overall. Fit as many as the page holds, chosen by relevance across the page. [MT: Mistakes, Significant, RU20]

## Accomplishment Bullet Formula: Verb → Result → Method

- **Verb, result, method.** Put the result up front and the method in a short trailing clause, often "by ...". The method is required and brief. [MT: Accomplishments, Experience, Managers]
- **Quantify the result.** Readers take an unmeasured result as small or failed. A project delivered on time, on budget, or in scope counts as measured without a digit; any one of the three is enough. One quantified bullet per project. [MT: Accomplishments, Experience, Stinks]
- **The method gives context** and draws interview questions: the tool, process, or team structure you used. Name a required technology inside an accomplishment, not in a skills list. [MT: Experience, Stinks, Myths]
- **One line each.** [MT: Accomplishments, Managers]
- **Managers claim the team's results.** Write "Achieved," not "we achieved," and give your method: "by leading ...", a practice, an initiative, coaching. [MT: Managers, Experience, Accomplishments]
- **Activity is never the result.** One-on-ones, training, coaching, and recruiting appear only as the method behind a business result. Recruiting on its own can signal turnover. [MT: Managers]
- **Fit the role's level.** A client's verdict counts as a result; a client's name alone doesn't. Learning counts only when tied to revenue or cost. [MT: Accomplishments]
- **Repeating the same structure is fine.** [MT: Accomplishments]

## Choosing Accomplishments

- Keep a Career Management Document with every accomplishment and its numbers, and pick from it for each target role. [MT: Accomplishments, Mistakes]
- **Relevance beats size.** Lead with an accomplishment that proves the target job's core purpose. [MT: Significant, RU20]
- **Significant** means harder than your own average, at that time, in that role. It doesn't have to be your single best. [MT: Significant]
- **Recency breaks ties.** An older, more relevant accomplishment beats a newer, less relevant one. [MT: Significant]

## Formatting

- Plain and professional: one font for the whole page, headings included. Bold sets off the admin line and appears nowhere else. No color, icons, images, graphs, shading, or borders. Use the full width of the page; empty space beside the text wastes it. [MT: Stinks, RU21, Myths, Mistakes] No underline anywhere on the page. The name and the admin line's bold segments print in Arial Bold; the rest of the page prints in Times. [Kyle]
- Type size never below 11pt, headings and contact line included, except the admin line's Arial Bold at 10.5pt, the size at which it matches 11pt Times. Never shrink type to force a fit. [Kyle]
- Body type no larger than 12pt. [House]

## Applying

- Send a cover letter or cover email. It matches your qualifications, required skills, and required certifications to the posting. [MT: RU20, RU22, Stinks]
- Don't optimize for keywords. An accurate description of the work supplies them, and ATS searches run broad. [MT: RU22, Myths, AI]
- Don't upload a PDF to an ATS, and check how the site renders what you upload. [MT: Mistakes]
- Proofread: check the dates on their own, and wait at least 12 hours between writing and editing. [MT: Mistakes]

## AI

Write the resume yourself. After that, AI can proofread (review each correction), suggest shorter wordings (expect to rework them into the resume's spare style), and suggest ways to quantify a role. Don't let AI draft the resume, and distrust AI feedback platforms. One person's AI tool, which wrote and sent applications on its own, got one interview from 500 applications. [MT: AI]

## How to Apply

Each job entry: admin line, responsibilities paragraph, then accomplishment bullets in verb/result/method form.

`scripts/lint-resume.mjs` checks each bullet for a measure, active voice, and no "we", plus the house banned-phrase list. `scripts/lib/resume-render.mjs` fails a print render when any bullet or admin line runs past one line or the page runs past one, and writes the .docx and .txt for ATS uploads from the same render. `resume-workflow/SKILL.md` governs editing cadence and register. `docs/features/resume-source.md` maps frontmatter fields to outputs.
